Shaping the sound

Drag any dot on the curve up or down to boost or cut that frequency band, from −25 to +25 dB. The left fader is overall volume, and the four sliders underneath are broad tone shelves layered on top of the 13 bands.

Every control is reachable from the keyboard: Tab to a dot, then arrow keys to move it. Home flattens that band.

Presets

Type a name and press Save. Pick a saved name from the box to load it, or press Delete to remove it. Bass Boost loads a warm low-end curve; Reset flattens everything back to zero.

Per-site on and off

The stop button is remembered per website — you can leave the EQ on for YouTube and off for Netflix. Volume keeps working either way.

If audio goes silent

Another audio extension has probably claimed the stream first, since a media element can only be captured once. Disable the other extension and reload the tab.

What this is

A 13-band Web Audio equalizer for any site that plays audio or video. It re-applies itself every time a new track starts, so your settings never reset between songs.

Privacy

The extension makes no network requests and collects nothing. Your curve is stored in Chrome's own synced settings so it follows your profile across devices.

Scope

EQ settings are global and shared across tabs; only the on/off switch is per-site.
